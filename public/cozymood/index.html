Cozy Mood – Help

Cozy Mood is a cosy pixel-art life sim by Artificials.net, created by Daril Pratomo Sriramdanu.

Write to us: support@artificials.net

Where are my saves?

On your device, in three slots with a backup of each. The game saves when you sleep, when you leave it and from the pause menu.

Does it need the internet?

No. Cozy Mood works entirely offline.

Is there anything to buy?

No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Languages and accessibility

The game speaks 15 languages (Settings → Language). Settings → Accessibility has colour filters, sound captions, text speed, easier fishing, left-handed controls and more.
