Cozy Mood – Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Cozy Mood is made by Artificials.net (Daril Pratomo Sriramdanu). This page explains what the game does with information about you.

In short: Cozy Mood collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it works entirely offline.

What stays on your device

Your saved games (three slots and their backups) and your settings (language, volume, accessibility options) are kept only on your device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

It does not connect to the internet. It does not collect, keep or share personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.

On Android the game may use vibration, to signal a fish biting. It asks for no other permission.

The game is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, children included.

App stores

Google Play and the App Store may collect information when you download the game, under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because an update adds an optional feature that needs a connection, the new version will appear here with a new date, and the update's notes will say so.
