Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. These are all built into the app and work without internet:

  • the whole Quran, with translations, transliteration, word-by-word and tajweed;
  • prayer times, qibla, duas and adhkar;
  • the prophets' stories and the prayer and Hajj guides.

Recitation audio, tafsir and the full hadith collections download when you first open them, and then work offline too. You can download whole surahs of audio in advance from Downloads.

How do I keep my progress when I change phones?

On iPhone, keep iCloud backup on (Me → iCloud backup) and sign in to the same Apple account on the new phone. On Android, turn on backup in your phone's settings; your progress returns when you install the app on the new phone with the same Google account.

Prayer times are a few minutes off from my mosque.

Open Prayer → Prayer settings. There you can:

  • choose the calculation method your country uses (for example Kemenag RI in Indonesia);
  • set the Asr method;
  • set your elevation;
  • adjust each prayer by a few minutes.
The adhan alert doesn't sound.

Check that notifications are allowed for Muslim Quran, and that the alert mode for that prayer isn't set to silent. On iPhone, a Focus mode can hide alerts. On Android, allow the app to run in the background in the battery settings.

How does Tadarus work?

Create a group, add the people reading with you, and the 30 juz are shared out evenly. Tick each juz when it's done, then tap Share progress to send a summary to your WhatsApp group. Members don't need the app.

How do I add a widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, and add Muslim Quran. On Android, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Widgets and choose Muslim Quran. Me → Widgets shows the steps.

I can't find Nuri, the assistant.

Nuri runs entirely on your phone, so it appears only on phones with an on-device model: iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, or Android phones with Gemini Nano. On iPhone, it currently needs the app in English, because Apple's model doesn't support Indonesian yet.

Pertanyaan yang sering diajukan

Apakah aplikasi bisa dipakai offline?

Bisa. Semua ini sudah ada di dalam aplikasi dan berjalan tanpa internet:

  • Al-Quran lengkap dengan terjemahan, latin, terjemahan per kata, dan tajwid;
  • jadwal sholat, kiblat, doa, dan dzikir;
  • kisah para nabi serta panduan sholat dan haji.

Audio murottal, tafsir, dan kitab hadis lengkap diunduh saat pertama dibuka, lalu bisa dipakai offline juga. Audio per surah bisa diunduh lebih dulu lewat menu Unduhan.

Bagaimana agar progres tidak hilang saat ganti HP?

Di iPhone, biarkan cadangan iCloud tetap aktif (Saya → Cadangan iCloud), lalu masuk dengan akun Apple yang sama di HP baru. Di Android, nyalakan cadangan di pengaturan HP; progresmu kembali saat aplikasi dipasang di HP baru dengan akun Google yang sama.

Jadwal sholat berbeda beberapa menit dengan masjid saya.

Buka Sholat → Pengaturan sholat. Di sana kamu bisa:

  • memilih metode perhitungan yang dipakai di negaramu (misalnya Kemenag RI di Indonesia);
  • mengatur metode Ashar;
  • mengisi ketinggian tempat;
  • menggeser waktu tiap sholat beberapa menit.
Adzan tidak berbunyi.

Pastikan notifikasi Muslim Quran diizinkan dan mode pengingat sholat tersebut tidak diatur senyap. Di iPhone, mode Fokus bisa menyembunyikan pengingat. Di Android, izinkan aplikasi berjalan di latar belakang lewat pengaturan baterai.

Bagaimana cara kerja Tadarus?

Buat grup dan tambahkan teman mengajimu, lalu ke-30 juz dibagi rata otomatis. Centang juz yang sudah selesai, lalu ketuk Bagikan progres untuk mengirim ringkasannya ke grup WhatsApp. Anggota tidak perlu memasang aplikasi.

Bagaimana cara memasang widget?

Di iPhone, sentuh lama Layar Utama atau Layar Kunci, ketuk Edit atau Sesuaikan, lalu tambahkan Muslim Quran. Di Android, sentuh lama Layar Utama, ketuk Widget, lalu pilih Muslim Quran. Langkah lengkapnya ada di Saya → Widget.

Saya tidak menemukan Nuri.

Nuri berjalan sepenuhnya di HP, jadi hanya muncul di perangkat yang punya model AI bawaan: iPhone dengan Apple Intelligence aktif, atau HP Android dengan Gemini Nano. Di iPhone, Nuri saat ini membutuhkan aplikasi berbahasa Inggris karena model Apple belum mendukung bahasa Indonesia.
